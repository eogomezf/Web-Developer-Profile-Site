Who I am

About me

I am a Systems Administrator and IT support engineer with 15+ years of experience supporting enterprise users, infrastructure, cloud environments, business applications and production systems. My work does not stop at “reboot the workstation”: I diagnose, escalate when needed, resolve, and close the loop with a root cause analysis and a corrective action that prevents the issue from coming back.

I am comfortable working with both technical and non-technical users: Active Directory, Microsoft Entra ID, Windows Server, Azure, AWS, DNS/DHCP, virtualization, PowerShell and Python are part of my daily work. I especially enjoy incident management, automating repetitive tasks and continuous improvement of operations.

I am a Senior Full-Stack Developer and Electronic Engineer. I build scalable web solutions with Angular, React and Next.js on top of Node.js, PHP and .NET backends, with PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQL Server, MongoDB and Firebase.

On top of writing code, I own the infrastructure that supports it: Linux and Windows Server environments, virtualization, CI/CD pipelines with Jenkins, Azure DevOps and SonarQube, and automation with Ansible. I lead agile teams under Scrum and I like the products we ship to be measurable in real operations.

On a personal note: I am an evangelical Christian and a servant of Jesus Christ; I love my family, my greatest treasure. I enjoy music, watching series and working out. I value teamwork and clear communication, because technology only works when people understand each other.
